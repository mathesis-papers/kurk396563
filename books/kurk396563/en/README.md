# From Stirling determinants to Schur polynomials: integer exponents and two OEIS conjectures

Haobo Ma (Chrono AI, Singapore) and Wenlin Zhang (Chrono AI, Singapore; National University of Singapore, Singapore) study an alternating nested sum that looks unstructured. It is actually a determinant of Stirling numbers, and that determinant is a rectangular Schur polynomial. This bridge explains every sign, factorial, and Vandermonde square, and extends the exponent from nonnegative integers to all integers.

The [story](story.md) introduces the question and mechanism with school algebra. The [precise statements](statements.md) then record all quantifiers, boundary cases, proof mechanisms, and the exact comparison with frozen Lean declarations. Only a complete statement match receives Lean evidence.

The source paper is public, version 2 (2026-11-05), under CC BY 4.0. It cites Merca’s 2014 determinant identities ([DOI 10.1007/s10998-014-0034-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10998-014-0034-3)) and Amdeberhan–Ekhad’s 2022 paper ([arXiv:2206.13244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13244v1)).
